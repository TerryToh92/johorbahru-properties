About Joey Koh

Joey Koh is a licensed Real Estate Negotiator (REN 77548) registered under GT Nelson Realty Sdn Bhd — specialising in Johor Bahru's new launch residential property market, with a particular focus on the CIQ corridor, Iskandar Puteri, and investment-grade developments.

With a client base spanning Singapore-based investors, expatriates, and first-time Malaysian buyers, Joey provides end-to-end guidance from project selection and developer briefings, through to loan facilitation, legal conveyancing coordination, and post-purchase rental management.

Fluent in English, Mandarin, and Bahasa Malaysia, Joey is equipped to serve buyers across language backgrounds and work with cross-border buyers navigating the Malaysia-Singapore property corridor.

Agency & Developer Relationship

Joey Koh operates as a licensed Real Estate Negotiator under GT Nelson Realty Sdn Bhd (E(1)1836) — a registered estate agency in Malaysia. GT Nelson Realty holds agency agreements with property developers to market their projects to buyers.

Joey Koh's role is to market, present and facilitate enquiries for new launch property projects on behalf of developers, and to provide independent buyer consultation and guidance throughout the purchase process.

Important: Joey Koh does not own, develop, construct, or act as the principal seller for any of the properties marketed on this website or on johorbahru-properties.com. All sales contracts, booking arrangements, developer warranties, and construction obligations remain the responsibility of the respective property developer. The authorization to market projects rests between the developer and GT Nelson Realty Sdn Bhd, not personally with Joey Koh.

What Joey Koh Does for Buyers

As a buyer's first point of contact, Joey provides:

  • Project briefings — layout, pricing, unit selection, and floor plan advice
  • Investment analysis — CIQ distance, rental yield context, and comparative value
  • Financing guidance — connecting buyers to bank loan advisors and legal panels
  • Booking coordination — facilitating the developer's official booking process
  • Post-purchase support — tenancy sourcing and property management referrals

All actual booking, sale and purchase agreements, and development warranties are issued by and remain the responsibility of the respective developer. Joey Koh facilitates the buyer-developer relationship but does not guarantee unit availability, loan approval, or project completion timelines.
