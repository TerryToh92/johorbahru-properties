Why Johor Bahru

The corridor that connects two cities.

Johor Bahru sits minutes from Singapore — two countries, one commute. With the RTS Link and the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone accelerating demand, property within walking distance of CIQ commands a significant location premium.

Land prices in JB remain approximately 5–10× cheaper per square foot than Singapore, while demand from Singapore-based commuters and investors has grown steadily as the RTS Link construction advances. All investment decisions should be based on independent financial and legal advice.

Sultan Iskandar CIQ

The main Malaysia-Singapore checkpoint. Properties within 1 km of the checkpoint benefit from strong location demand and transit connectivity.

RTS Link Station

Rapid Transit System connecting JB Sentral to Woodlands North — under construction, due to open. Projects near the JB Sentral station benefit from proximity to this major new transit hub.

JB City Centre

The commercial core — JBCC, Danga Bay, Stulang — where lifestyle, transit, and investment converge. Most featured projects are here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying property in Johor Bahru.

Johor Bahru is undergoing a historic transformation with the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), the RTS Link, and Forest City developments driving economic activity, while land prices remain significantly lower than Singapore.
Properties in Johor Bahru can be purchased at roughly 5 to 10 times less per square foot than Singapore while sharing the same geographic proximity, and demand is rising as more Singaporean professionals commute via the RTS Link.
CIQ stands for Customs, Immigration and Quarantine, the checkpoint zone at the Malaysia-Singapore border. Properties near CIQ benefit from unmatched connectivity to Singapore and demand growth from the upcoming RTS Link.
Properties within 1 to 3 kilometres of the CIQ corridor may appeal to buyers and tenants because of their location and cross-border connectivity. However, actual capital appreciation and rental yields vary by development, purchase price, unit type, rental demand, operating expenses and market conditions. Past performance does not guarantee future returns. Buyers should independently verify market data and conduct financial and legal due diligence before purchasing.
