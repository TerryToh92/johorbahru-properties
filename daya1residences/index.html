Project 1 · Daya 1 Residences · Freehold Condo
Daya 1 Residences

Freehold Living in Mature Taman Daya

Daya 1 Residences is a freehold serviced apartment by the established Keck Seng Group, set in the mature and connected township of Taman Daya, Johor Bahru. With 852 units across 2 towers of 35 levels, it delivers modern high-rise living in one of JB's most established neighbourhoods.

Rare freehold tenure — permanent ownership in an established Johor Bahru township, among the few freehold new launches available in the Tebrau corridor.

Level 4 Multi-Generational Deck — a dedicated facilities floor designed for residents of all ages, from children's play to adult recreation and senior-friendly spaces.

PES units available — select Type A3 and B2 units include a Private Enclosed Space, extending your home with an exclusive outdoor deck.

Unbeatable connectivity — direct access via the EDL, Tebrau Highway, and Pasir Gudang Highway, with IKEA Tebrau and AEON Tebrau City nearby.

Trusted developer — built by Keck Seng (Malaysia) Berhad, a reputable group with decades of experience in quality Johor Bahru property development.

Greenwoods Residence

Exclusive Gated Living in Taman Daya

Greenwoods Residence is a rare freehold landed development by Keck Seng in Taman Daya — offering just 106 exclusive units of cluster homes, semi-detached houses, and link bungalows within a private, low-density gated and guarded community.

Freehold with individual title — each landed unit carries its own freehold individual title, offering permanent ownership of both land and property.

Gated & Guarded community — a secure, controlled-access estate with perimeter fencing and round-the-clock guard services for residents' peace of mind.

Only 106 units — exclusive & low density — an intimate community of just over 100 homes, ensuring privacy, exclusivity, and a refined neighbourhood atmosphere.

Private garden & yard — every landed unit comes with its own private outdoor space — perfect for family gatherings, pet ownership, or greenery.

Phase 1 CCC received — Phase 1 is fully constructed and CCC has been issued. Phase 2 is nearing completion — a tangible, near-ready development you can visit and inspect today.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the two Keck Seng projects in Taman Daya?
Daya 1 Residences is a freehold high-rise serviced apartment comprising 852 units across 2 towers, with 6 unit types from 590–954 sqft, PES units, and a Level 4 Multi-Gen Facilities Deck. Greenwoods Residence is a freehold gated and guarded landed development of 106 exclusive units — cluster homes, semi-detached houses, and link bungalows — with Phase 1 CCC received and Phase 2 nearing completion. Both are by Keck Seng (Malaysia) Berhad in Taman Daya, Johor Bahru.
Are both Daya 1 Residences and Greenwoods Residence freehold?
Yes — both developments are freehold. Daya 1 Residences is a freehold serviced apartment, and Greenwoods Residence is freehold with individual land titles for each landed unit. Freehold properties in Johor Bahru are increasingly rare, and both developments represent permanent ownership opportunities in an established township.
What is the completion status of Greenwoods Residence?
Phase 1 of Greenwoods Residence has been fully constructed and CCC has been received. Phase 2 is nearing completion. Buyers can visit and inspect the development on site before committing. Contact Joey Koh (REN 77548) via WhatsApp at +60125686285 for the latest project update and unit availability — no buyer agent fee applies.
What is a PES unit in Daya 1 Residences?
A PES (Private Enclosed Space) unit includes an exclusive private outdoor deck or garden extension attached to the unit — adding usable living area beyond the indoor built-up. At Daya 1 Residences, PES units are available in Type A3 (721 sqft, 1 Bed + Study) and Type B2 (850 sqft, 2 Bed). This is a popular feature that brings a landed-like outdoor lifestyle to a high-rise setting.
Is Taman Daya a good location in Johor Bahru?
Yes — Taman Daya is one of JB's most mature and established townships in the Tebrau corridor. It offers complete amenities including IKEA Tebrau, AEON Tebrau City, Columbia Asia Hospital, schools, supermarkets, and excellent highway connectivity via the EDL, Tebrau Highway, and North-South Expressway. Both Daya 1 Residences and Greenwoods Residence benefit from this prime Tebrau location.
