FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Iconic by PGB?
The Iconic by PGB is a freehold serviced apartment development by PGB Iconic Sdn Bhd (a wholly-owned subsidiary of Paragon Globe Berhad, listed on Bursa Malaysia's Main Market). Located at Jalan Indera Putera, Stulang Darat, Johor Bahru, it features twin towers with 1,510 units total and 52 planned facilities across approximately 1.85 acres. The development is approximately 2.0 km by road to Bukit Chagar RTS and 1.9–2.0 km to CIQ.
Is The Iconic freehold?
Yes. The Iconic is a freehold development under commercial title. Buyers hold permanent ownership with no leasehold expiry. The property is classified as serviced apartments — commercial title with no retail units.
How far is The Iconic from CIQ and Bukit Chagar RTS?
The Iconic is approximately 1.9–2.0 km by road to CIQ and approximately 2.0 km by road to Bukit Chagar RTS Link station. JB Sentral is approximately 1.8 km away, and the Berjaya Waterfront Ferry Terminal is approximately 1.9 km. These are approximate road distances from project materials — they are not travel times; actual commute will vary with traffic and checkpoint queues.
What unit types are available in Tower A?
Tower A offers three layouts: Type A (526 sqft, 1 bedroom/1 bathroom, 251 units, proposed RM578,600–RM619,600), Type B (756 sqft, 2 bedrooms/2 bathrooms), and Type C (956 sqft, dual-key configuration). All types include a partial furnishing package: 5-star-rated air conditioners (Daikin or equivalent), ceiling fans (KDK or equivalent), digital lock (Sharp or equivalent), kitchen cabinet, and kitchen hood & hob (Electrolux or equivalent). Brands and specifications are indicative.
Can foreigners buy at The Iconic?
Yes. Foreign buyers are eligible to purchase units priced at RM500,000 and above. The published Standard Package includes free legal fees on SPA & LA and free stamp duty on loan disbursement. For the first 50 unit bookings, the developer contributes to the Johor state levy — the buyer pays RM25,000 with the developer covering the balance, subject to SPA & LA signing within 30 days from APDL or the developer's signing date. Eligibility and levy subsidy availability are subject to current regulations and the developer's terms.
What facilities does The Iconic have?
The Iconic plans 52 facilities across ~1.85 acres: 42 at Level 11 and 10 on the rooftop. Level 11 highlights include a thriving lap pool, jacuzzi, aqua gym, kids growing pool, padel court, gym, fitness centre, sauna, golf simulator, entertainment room, co-office lounge, study room, BBQ area, gourmet kitchen, contemplative garden, and kids room. The rooftop features 4 pickleball courts, edible garden, kids adventure zone, sunrise deck, and the Canopy. Continuous ramps are planned for wheelchair and stroller accessibility throughout.
What is the shuttle service at The Iconic?
A complimentary shuttle is planned between The Iconic and JB Sentral, with more frequent service during morning and evening peak hours (every 20–25 minutes from 5:30 am–9:30 am and 4:30 pm–8:30 pm). Off-peak runs include R&F Mall stops. The proposed schedule is for reference only — times, stops and frequency may change and do not form a contractual obligation.
When is The Iconic expected to complete?
The Iconic targets completion within 60 months after the Sale & Purchase Agreement is signed. Completion timing is a target and remains subject to confirmation in the Sale & Purchase Agreement.
What is Aurum Stay at The Iconic?
Aurum Stay is a hospitality management provider planned for The Iconic, offering 24-hour front desk reception, professional housekeeping, interior design consultation, booking-platform support, private transport by advance booking, and resident housekeeping/handyman services. Services, fees and availability are subject to Aurum Stay's terms and may change. Management arrangements should be independently reviewed before purchase.
