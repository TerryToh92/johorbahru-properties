About KSL D'Secret Garden 2 (Pangsapuri Kempas Indah)

KSL D'Secret Garden 2, officially registered as Pangsapuri Kempas Indah with PKPJ Johor Bahru, is a freehold high-rise serviced apartment development by KSL Holdings Berhad (Bursa Malaysia: KSL 5038) located in Kempas Indah, Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia. The project is situated directly adjacent to the North-South Highway at Exit 255 (Kempas Interchange), providing immediate highway access and a 15-minute drive to the Johor Bahru CIQ (Customs, Immigration and Quarantine) checkpoint via the toll-free Eastern Dispersal Link (EDL) Highway.

The development comprises studio, 2-bedroom, and 3-bedroom units ranging from 506 sqft to 893 sqft. Prices start from RM254,000 for studio units (Type A), RM328,000 for 2-bedroom units (Type B: 667 sqft / Type B1: 753 sqft), and RM438,000 for 3-bedroom units (Type C). The project carries freehold tenure with individual strata title, zero legal fees on both the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) and loan agreement, a maintenance fee of RM0.10 per sqft per month with a 1-year waiver upon handover, and an estimated completion date of 2027 Q1.

Key nearby landmarks include: AEON Mall Bandar Dato' Onn (5 min), KDN Immigration Department (opposite), Senai International Airport (20 min), KSL City Mall, Paradigm Mall JB, Mid Valley Southkey, Johor Premium Outlets (JPO), IKEA Tebrau, Toppen Shopping Centre, KPJ Hospital Bandar Dato' Onn, Kempas Medical Centre, and Southern College University.
