Frequently Asked Questions

KSL Lakeview — Everything You Need to Know

What is KSL Lakeview?
KSL Lakeview is a freehold serviced apartment by KSL Holdings Berhad in Ponderosa Township, Taman Molek, Johor Bahru. It offers 1,200 units across 35 levels with 6 unit types from Studio to 3-Bedroom (506–883 sqft). Priced from RM 260,000, targeting completion in 2028, and open to foreign buyers.
Where is KSL Lakeview located?
KSL Lakeview sits within Ponderosa Township in Taman Molek, Johor Bahru — approximately 15 minutes by car to the JB CIQ checkpoint to Singapore. It is close to KSL City Mall, AEON Tebrau City, IKEA Tebrau, Mid Valley Southkey, and Ponderosa Golf & Country Club.
Is KSL Lakeview freehold?
Yes. KSL Lakeview is fully freehold (永久地契), meaning buyers hold permanent land ownership with no leasehold expiry. Freehold new launches remain relatively rare in centrally located Johor Bahru townships, and KSL Holdings has established a track record of freehold developments in the area.
What unit types and sizes are available?
Six layouts are offered: Type A Studio (506 sqft), Type B1 1+1 Bedroom (646 sqft), Type B2 2 Bedrooms (746 sqft), Type B3 1+1 Bedroom with Outdoor Deck (646+237 sqft), Type B4 2 Bedrooms with Outdoor Deck (764+355 sqft), and Type C 3 Bedrooms (883 sqft).
What is the starting price for KSL Lakeview?
Prices start from RM 260,000 (approximately RM 514 per sqft) for a Studio unit. Contact Joey Koh (REN 77548) for the latest price list, unit availability, and floor plans — no buyer agent fee applies.
Who is the developer of KSL Lakeview?
KSL Lakeview is developed by KSL Holdings Berhad (Bursa Malaysia: 5038), a publicly listed Malaysian property group known for large integrated townships in Johor Bahru, including KSL City and the Ponderosa community. Developer Advertising License: 3390/03/2029/0030(A). Verified at teduh.kpkt.gov.my.
What facilities does KSL Lakeview offer?
KSL Lakeview features 11 facilities: Swimming Pool, Wading Pool, BBQ Area, Jogging Track, Gymnasium, Outdoor Gym, Multi-Purpose Hall, Children Playground, EV Charging Station, Multi-tier Security System, and a Shuttle Bus to KSL City Mall.
What schools are near KSL Lakeview?
Nearby schools include SJK(C) Johor Jaya (4km), SJK(C) Pandan (6km), Paragon Private & International School (6km), Austin Heights Private & International School (9km), and Sunway College Johor Bahru (10km).
Can foreigners or Singapore buyers purchase KSL Lakeview?
Yes. KSL Lakeview is open to foreign buyers. Singapore citizens, permanent residents, and other foreign nationals may purchase subject to the prevailing minimum purchase price threshold set by the Johor state government.
