About the Development

Freehold Landed Living
by KSL Holdings

Pulai Bestari is a gated and guarded freehold landed community by KSL Holdings Berhad in Taman Universiti, Skudai. Offering two types of landed homes — double storey terrace and cluster — within a private, low-density environment.

Gated & Guarded — 24-hour security with access control for residents and visitors.

KSL Holdings Berhad — a publicly listed property developer (Bursa: KSL) with a strong track record of large-scale township development in Johor Bahru.

Individual Title — each unit comes with its own individual strata or land title, giving buyers full ownership rights over their property.

Open to Foreign Buyers — both P4 and P2A phases are eligible for foreign purchase subject to Johor state guidelines and minimum pricing thresholds.

Established Township — located within Taman Universiti, a mature and well-served neighbourhood near UTM, with schools, shops and highways nearby.
