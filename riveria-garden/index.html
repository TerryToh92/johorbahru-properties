Riveria Garden @ Wawari is a 1,026-acre freehold integrated township in Iskandar Puteri, Johor, developed by Wawari Sdn Bhd (a member of KSL Group / KSL Holdings Berhad). It offers four freehold property types: Riverhaus serviced apartments, Riveria Garden Avenia double storey terrace, Elora Cluster & Semi-D homes, and RiverPoint retail shops.
Yes. All four property types are freehold. The Avenia terrace, Elora cluster/semi-D, and RiverPoint retail shops carry individual freehold titles. Riverhaus serviced apartments carry a Commercial (HDA) freehold title. All buyers hold permanent ownership with no leasehold expiry.
Approximately 15 minutes by car to the CIQ 2nd Link (Tuas Second Link checkpoint). Riveria Garden @ Wawari has direct access to the 2nd Link Expressway, making it one of the most conveniently Singapore-connected residential townships in Johor Bahru.

3. Elora Cluster & Semi-D — Luxury landed, 250 units, 32×70 ft, from RM 1,318,000, completion 2028.
