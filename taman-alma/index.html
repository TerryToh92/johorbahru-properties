Common Questions

Taman Alma is a KSL development area in Skudai, Johor Bahru, featuring two freehold projects: Ceros Residences (serviced apartment) and Alma Rosewood (double storey terrace), both developed by KSL Holdings Berhad — a Bursa Malaysia-listed developer.
Yes, both Ceros Residences and Alma Rosewood are freehold. Ceros Residences carries a Commercial (HDA) freehold title, while Alma Rosewood carries an individual freehold title. Buyers hold permanent ownership with no leasehold expiry.
Yes, both projects are open to foreign buyers, subject to Johor state guidelines and minimum purchase price thresholds. Contact Joey Koh for the latest eligibility requirements before committing.
Ceros Residences is a high-rise serviced apartment (1,200 units, 30 levels) starting from RM 245,000 — ideal for investors and first-time buyers seeking an affordable freehold unit. Alma Rosewood is a landed double storey terrace (250 units, 20×65/70 ft) starting from RM 828,000 — suited for families seeking a freehold landed home in Skudai.
No buyer agent fee applies. For new launch properties, agent commission is paid entirely by the developer. You pay the same developer price whether you enquire through an agent or directly — and benefit from full advisory, unit selection, and financing referral at no cost.
