The Development

A Modern Residence in Johor's Premier SEZ

Verte Medini Residence is a modern high-rise development thoughtfully designed for contemporary urban living in the heart of Iskandar Medini — one of Malaysia's most progressive smart city townships within the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone.

Strategic location in Iskandar Puteri — minutes from the upcoming Johor–Singapore RTS Link, providing a 5-minute rail connection to Woodlands North MRT, Singapore from 2026.

Seamless connectivity via the Iskandar Coastal Highway, North–South Expressway, and Malaysia–Singapore Second Link — with direct access to Singapore.

Lifestyle amenities include a swimming pool, gymnasium, landscaped gardens, children's play areas, and multipurpose community spaces.

Surrounded by world-class facilities — Medini Mall, Sunway BigBox, LEGOLAND® Malaysia, Gleneagles Hospital Medini, and Marlborough College Malaysia.

Free inclusions — every unit comes with one free parking lot and partial furnishing package.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What unit types are available at Verte Medini Residence?
Verte Medini offers 5 unit types with built-up areas ranging from 614 sqft to 935 sqft. Configurations include 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, 3-bedroom, and dual-key layouts — suitable for own stay, small families, or rental investment. Free parking is included with every unit, and all units come partial furnished.
What is the location of Verte Medini and what is nearby?
Verte Medini is located in Iskandar Medini, Iskandar Puteri, Johor. Key landmarks nearby include Medini Mall, Sunway BigBox, LEGOLAND® Malaysia, Gleneagles Hospital Medini, Marlborough College Malaysia, and the Medini Business District. The development benefits from direct 2nd Link connectivity to Singapore.
Can Singapore buyers or foreigners purchase Verte Medini?
Yes. Singapore citizens, PRs, and foreigners are eligible to purchase property in Iskandar Medini. The minimum purchase price threshold for foreigners in Johor is generally RM600,000, and properties in the Medini SEZ may qualify for additional incentives. Contact Joey for full eligibility details relevant to your nationality.
Is there a buyer agent fee if I enquire through this page?
No buyer agent fee applies. Agent commission is paid entirely by the developer upon a successful transaction. You pay the same developer price whether you walk in directly or enquire through us — but with us you get full advisory support, unit selection guidance, and loan referral at no cost.
How does the RTS Link affect this property?
The Johor–Singapore RTS Link, targeted for completion in 2026, will connect Bukit Chagar JB to Woodlands North MRT in approximately 5 minutes. Combined with the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), this is expected to significantly increase demand and property values in the Iskandar corridor, of which Iskandar Medini is a key precinct.
